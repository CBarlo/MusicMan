MusicMan Load-In Guide
Physical setup & breakdown for a campfire show — cases, cabling, and the order things happen in.
Field Copy
This is for getting boxes off the truck, gear on the ground, cables connected, and everything packed back up correctly. It doesn't cover running the show — that's MusicMan_Manual.html.

New to this? You don't need to know how any of it works. Every step below is one concrete action — read it, do that one thing, then move to the next. If a step genuinely doesn't make sense standing in front of the gear, stop and ask rather than guess — most of this only works done in order.

Real-world timing: one adult working solo — poles, Music Man Box, and PA (not counting the EZ-Up, screen, projector, or generator) — took about 1 hour 20 minutes. A second or third person on cables/poles while one runs the console table cuts that down a lot. Not a one-person job if you can help it.

Per-Pole Assembly

Both poles are identical. Finish one completely before starting the second — working on both at once is how cables get crossed.

Wash Bar sits at the bottom of the pole with the Node. The long cable carries the signal up the pole to the Pinspot/PAR/PAR cluster at the top, where short cables link the three.

  1. Raise the pole to full extensionat its marked position. Extend fully before mounting anything — the pixel strip receivers only line up with the strips' magnets at full extension.
  2. Set the Solix C300at the base. Confirm it's charged and powered on before connecting anything to it.
  3. Power the Noderun the 12V cable from the Solix's DC output into the Pole Node.
  4. Connect the pixel snakeplug the snake into the Node's aircraft connector. Match each of the 5 numbered tails to its numbered strip — number to number. Velcro the bundle near the Node's power port so nothing hangs loose.
  5. Wire the DMX chainNode → Wash Bar → Pinspot → PAR → PAR, per the diagram above.
  6. Power the fixturessee the power map below — wash bar and pinspot both draw off the Solix, PARs are battery-only.
  7. Mount & aimWash bar — bottom of the pole, on its hook, secured with a wingnut, general stage coverage. Pinspot + both PARs — top of the pole, same hook + wingnut mounting; PARs wash the stage generally, the pinspot is a tight aim at dead center, dad/kid standing height. The 5 pixel strips (2 stage-facing, 3 audience-facing) snap on via magnet mounts into their receivers — if one doesn't land, check the pole is fully extended before assuming something's broken.

Site Layout

A
In the audience, close to stage
Both poles sit in the audience area, near the stage.
B
On the stage/audience line
Both poles sit on the boundary line between stage and audience, spread apart from each other.

Pick whichever fits the site. Either way, fixture aim stays the same: PARs and wash bars wash the stage generally, pinspots hit dead center at dad/kid standing height.

EZ-Up — the big Expedition EZ-Up is the stage backdrop and the backstage/prep area behind it, not a console tent. Raise it early; the console table sets up separately. Screen faces the seating area with a clear, reasonably short projector throw — the ~100ft Cat6 run covers the rest of the distance back to the console, so the console doesn't need to be anywhere near the projector. PA speakers go on stands, spread apart, facing the crowd. No fixed distances beyond that — read the site and adjust.

Power

⚠

Screen inflator needs continuous power — not a one-time inflate-and-disconnect. It stays plugged in and running the whole time the screen needs to stay up, or it sags/collapses.

Shore power vs. generator
Use shore power if the site has it. Generator only comes out as backup, with no shore power available.
Music Man Box
Needs power at the console, from shore/generator.
Projector
Has its own battery, but plug it in when power's available — don't rely on battery for a full show.
Video extender
Needs power at one end only — console or projector side, whichever's more convenient.

Setup
6

Setup Order

  1. Site prepwalk the site, confirm the fire circle, mark pole / EZ-Up / screen / PA positions.
  2. Raise the EZ-Upit's the stage backdrop and backstage/prep area, not a console tent. The console table sets up separately, later.
  3. Check for shore powerif none, position the generator downwind/away from seating, confirm gas, start it, let it settle before loading anything onto it.
  4. Set up the folding tablewhere the console will go, under/near the EZ-Up. Everything in the Music Man Box and the PA controls end up here.
  5. Position the screenfacing the seating area.
  6. Inflate the screenplug in the inflator and confirm it's actually running continuously before moving on — it stays plugged in and blowing the whole show, not a one-time inflate. Don't walk away and unplug it thinking it's done.
  7. Position the projectorreasonably short, clear throw to the screen.
  8. Run the long Cat6 cablefrom the projector back to the console.
  9. Connect the video/HDMI extendera pair of small boxes — one end plugs into the projector's HDMI input, the other into the console's HDMI output, with the long Cat6 running between them. Power whichever end is more convenient — only one end needs power.
  10. Set up each polefull per-pole assembly (Section 2), one pole completely before starting the second. Use the step stool to reach the fixtures for aiming — don't aim from the ground by eye.
  11. Set up the PA (Fender Venue)full sequence above: speakers on stands, wireless receiver powered and paired, everything cabled in. Leave the Venue itself off until every source is connected.
  12. Set up the Music Man Boxon the console table: Pi unit, Stream Deck, remote, iPad mini, wired mic + stand, wireless Rx (power + pair the 2 wireless mics, fresh AA batteries), MusicMan cable into the Venue.
  13. Power onshore/generator first, then the Venue, then Solix units, MusicMan unit last.
  14. Verify connectivityPi status light: amber (booting) → blue (running) → green once both poles check in. Not green usually means a loose cable, not a real failure — worth a look before assuming the worst.
  15. Walk the Show Flow, live, on the consolestep through every entry from the start, confirming each one actually fires the right music/light/video before the audience shows up. The best way to catch a loose cable or wrong setting while it's still easy to fix.
  16. Hand offto the show operator for a final check of lights, sound, and display before the show starts.
Breakdown
7

Breakdown Order

⚠

Shut the Pi down before touching its power. Press the power button once and wait for the status light to go solid white — that's a few seconds, not instant — before unplugging anything. White means it actually finished shutting down; cutting power earlier risks corrupting the SD card.

  1. Pack the Music Man Boxmics off, batteries out if it'll sit a while, everything back in its case slots.
  2. Power down each polefixtures off, then Node, then Solix. Disconnect in reverse: PAR→PAR, PAR→Pinspot, Pinspot→Wash Bar, Wash Bar→Node.
  3. Coil every cable individuallynot as one tangled bundle — the fastest way to turn a quick teardown into a slow one next time.
  4. Lower and pack each polefixtures and pixel strips secured so nothing swings loose in the case.
  5. PA, screen, EZ-Updown in that order.
  6. Generatorlet it cool before packing; confirm the gas can is sealed.
  7. Final sweepwalk the site for anything left behind before loading the truck.
